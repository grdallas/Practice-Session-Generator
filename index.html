About the AI

Sessions are generated by an AI model, not looked up from a verified source. It's built to double-check its own work on things like tempo math, chord tones, and physically playable technique — but it can still occasionally get a specific detail wrong, especially on less common instruments or more obscure theory. Treat anything technical (a scale choice, an interval, a fingering) as worth a moment's sanity check before you build a habit around it, the same way you would with any other single source.

AI Model — Testing Only

Applies to every AI call — generating, swapping, adjusting, and asking. This is here for comparing quality/cost while testing, not a real user-facing setting yet.

Backup & Restore

Your profile, goals, skills, custom instructions, and practice log all live only in this specific browser — they won't follow you to a different browser, computer, or copy of this file. Export a backup periodically, and import it to restore everything (or carry it somewhere else).
